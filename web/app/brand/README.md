# NOAI brand

The mark is a closed eye inside a ring: a dot above, and seven lashes glowing under the lowered lid. The product works with its eyes closed. Confirmed by Ramzi on 27 Sep 2026 (the orange cover), replacing the 26 Sep lidded lens.

- `noai-mark.svg`: the full mark in orange (disc #ED3200, black). Use at 64 px and above.
- `noai-mark-blue.svg`: the same mark in Ramzi's blue version (disc #34C3E8, white rings). The purple themes use it.
- `noai-mark-small.svg`: the small cut, thinner lines and five lashes, rings in `currentColor`. Use below 64 px.
- `noai-mark-small-blue.svg`: the small cut in blue.
- `noai-wordmark.svg`: NOAI in Montserrat Light, outlined and converted to paths, stroke in `currentColor`.
- `favicon.svg` / `favicon-blue.svg`: the small cut on a rounded square, orange or blue. `theme.js` switches to the blue one for the purple themes.

Colours: orange field #FC481C, disc #ED3200, ink #000. Blue field #64E0F8, disc #34C3E8, rings #FFF, dot #0A8CFF.
The full kit (lockups, app icons, PNG sizes, favicon.ico) is in Downloads as "NOAI - Logo Kit (orange).zip".
